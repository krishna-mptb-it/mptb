Madhya Pradesh State Tourism Awards

Terms & Conditions for Submission of Entries

These Terms & Conditions ("Terms") govern the submission of entries/nominations for the Madhya Pradesh State Tourism Awards ("Awards") instituted to recognise excellence and outstanding contribution to the tourism sector of Madhya Pradesh. By submitting an entry, the applicant ("Applicant") agrees to be bound by these Terms in full.

1. Eligibility

  1. The Awards are open to individuals, organisations, hotels, resorts, tour operators, travel agencies, homestays, government bodies, and other tourism-related establishments operating within the State of Madhya Pradesh, unless otherwise specified for a particular award category.
  2. The Applicant must possess all valid statutory registrations, licences, and permissions required to operate in the tourism sector, where applicable.
  3. Entities blacklisted, under active litigation concerning tourism operations, or found in violation of applicable tourism/hospitality regulations may be deemed ineligible at the sole discretion of the Department of Tourism, Government of Madhya Pradesh ("the Department").

2. Submission Requirements

  1. Entries must be submitted in the prescribed format, along with all requisite supporting documents, within the notified submission window. Late, incomplete, or improperly formatted entries are liable to be rejected without further correspondence.
  2. All information, data, statistics, and documents furnished must be true, accurate, and complete to the best of the Applicant's knowledge. Any false, misleading, or fabricated information may result in immediate disqualification.
  3. Applicants may apply in only one (1) category unless the guidelines for a specific Award cycle expressly permit multiple category submissions.
  4. Supporting materials (photographs, videos, testimonials, certificates, etc.) submitted must be owned by the Applicant or used with proper authorisation. The Applicant is solely responsible for ensuring no third-party rights are infringed.

3. Evaluation and Selection

  1. All entries will be evaluated by a Jury/Selection Committee constituted by the Department, based on criteria notified for each Award category.
  2. The decision of the Jury/Selection Committee shall be final and binding on all Applicants. No correspondence, appeal, or challenge shall be entertained regarding the evaluation, scoring, or final outcome.
  3. The Department reserves the right to conduct physical verification, site visits, or seek additional clarification/documents from any Applicant prior to finalisation of Awards.
  4. The Department reserves the right to withhold, cancel, or not confer an Award in any category if, in its sole judgment, no entry meets the requisite standard.

4. Fees

Unless expressly stated otherwise in the specific Award notification, submission of entries shall be free of cost. Where an application/processing fee is applicable, the same shall be non-refundable under all circumstances, including disqualification or non-selection.

  2. The Applicant warrants that it holds full rights to all submitted content and shall indemnify the Department against any third-party claims arising from such use.

6. Disqualification

The Department reserves the right to disqualify any entry, at any stage — including after conferment of an Award — if it is found that:

  • The submitted information or documents are false, forged, or misleading;
  • The Applicant has engaged in unethical practices, canvassing, or attempted to influence the Jury;

These Terms shall be governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with these Terms or the Awards shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts at Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh.
